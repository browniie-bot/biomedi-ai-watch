## LinkedIn

BioMed AI Watch, Oct 25 – Nov 1: this week's AI was judged on more than AUROC.

• TRACER, an EHR foundation model (arXiv preprint), kept its discrimination in health systems held out from development. At 95% specificity, its alert burden still varied by disease and site.
• Local updates to diabetic retinopathy AI improved probability scores but sharply raised patient flags, and net benefit sometimes fell (medRxiv preprint).
• Ask Eolas, an antimicrobial advice tool available in UK hospitals, handled simple queries well but gave some potentially unsafe answers in complex cases (preprint).
• In Nature Medicine, EVLF-FM pairs diagnosis across 10 imaging modalities with pixel-level visual grounding.

The gap we flag: before a locally updated model goes live, it should be checked for patient-level net benefit and alert burden, not just calibration.
