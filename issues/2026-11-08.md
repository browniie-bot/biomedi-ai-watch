## Gaps in this week's research

1. **Unknown how well deployed ECG AI performs in adults aged 75+** Preprint: 30-day MACE AUROC was 0.64-0.71 at age 75+ versus 0.81-0.90 under 40. This week's cardiovascular 510(k) records report no age-group results. ([1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.30.26366943v1), [2](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K260640), [3](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K252736)) _Study:_ Age-stratified external validation of cleared ECG algorithms in older-adult cohorts, with post-market subgroup reporting and recalibration targets.
2. **No real-time warning, before outcomes are known, of AI performance loss after transfer** After transfer to eICU, AUROC fell to 0.76-0.77. No uncertainty measure flagged the loss, and missed deaths carried low uncertainty. ([1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.03.26367064v1)) _Study:_ Prospective multi-site study comparing uncertainty, input-drift and rapid outcome-label audits for detecting degradation of transferred mortality models.
3. **Public clinical benchmarks lack patient-level split and label-provenance audits** Record-level MIMIC-CXR splits and AQ-10 labels built from the input items inflated accuracy. Q1-journal AUCs cluster at round thresholds. ([1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07950v1), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.01.26367003v1), [3](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.02.26367135v1)) _Study:_ Re-audit the most-cited public clinical datasets for leakage and circular labels, then publish corrected splits and re-scored leaderboards.
