## Research

**Validation beyond headline metrics.** Studies test screening thresholds, local updates, calibration under shift and abstention. Several show that good AUROC can hide alert burden or miscalibration. ([1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40536v1), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.23.26366103v1), [3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39065v1), [4](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.27.26366616v1))

**LLMs measured against guidelines and clinicians.** Benchmarks and head-to-head comparisons are multiplying. Guideline-structured methods help, but complex cases expose unsafe outputs, and many evaluations rely on synthetic cases or simulated patients. ([1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.27.26366650v1), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.22.26366212v1), [3](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.23.26366291v1), [4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40476v1), [5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40327v1), [6](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.23.26366376v1))

**Explainable and modality-specific foundation models.** Explainability comes from pixel grounding and readable morphology. Models are pretrained for micro-ultrasound and PET. An independent fMRI audit found released models hard to reuse. ## Gaps in this week's research

1. **Local model updates lack patient-level net-benefit and alert-burden checks before rollout** Retinal updates improved Brier loss everywhere yet raised mBRSET flags to 809–951 per 1,000. TRACER alert burden varied by site. No tabular model withstood event-rate shift. ([1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.23.26366103v1), [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40536v1), [3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39065v1)) _Study:_ Multisite update protocol with preset net-benefit and flags-per-detection thresholds, comparing recalibration options before any locally updated screening model goes live.
2. **Clinical LLM tools are validated on LLM-written cases, scored by LLM judges** _(recurring)_ The NIHSS benchmark used LLM-written transcripts and answer keys. Ask Eolas was tested on generated queries with an LLM judge. The sepsis comparison used six vignettes. ([1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.23.26366376v1), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.22.26366212v1), [3](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.23.26366291v1)) _Study:_ Score NIHSS from recorded real code-stroke exams against certified examiners; audit Ask Eolas on logged hospital queries with clinician adjudication.
3. **Released medical foundation models are hard for independent researchers to reuse** BrainLM and BrainHarmonix both needed substantial code changes to run on Prevent-AD. PedDx shows an external site can independently run a full workflow. ([1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.22.26366227v1), [2](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.27.26366616v1)) _Study:_ Independent reproduction audits of published imaging foundation models on new clinical cohorts, reporting setup effort and gains over simple baselines.
